Historical migration

One-time import for your canonical history file. Budget Margin keeps the original source marker so migrated records can be distinguished from day-to-day entries.

Month-end reconciliation

Import a bank/card CSV or text-based PDF, compare it with Budget Margin, review anything unmatched, then close the month. CSV works fully offline; PDF extraction needs internet for the pinned PDF.js parser.

iPhone capture bridge

Recommended iOS 27 flow: Shortcut copies the bank notification to the clipboard. Open Budget Margin from your Home Screen, then tap Paste payment. Budget Margin reads the clipboard directly, parses the payment and opens a prefilled confirmation sheet — no text box required. URL capture remains available as a fallback.
